Explain Sections

Project Overview

Ye project hai kya?

Main Idea

Ye ek smart dashboard hai jo campus ke energy, water, waste aur solar data ko ek jagah laakar live analysis, AI prediction aur reporting deta hai.

Kis ke liye useful hai?

Management, sustainability cell, estate team, hostel administration, audit team aur decision makers ke liye useful hai.

Expected benefit

Decision fast hota hai, wastage identify hoti hai, forecast milta hai aur report presentation-ready form mein milti hai.

Problem

Campus ko iski zarurat kyun hai?

Data scattered hota hai

Energy alag sheet mein, water alag report mein, waste manually maintain hota hai, aur solar alag source mein hota hai. Isse combined decision mushkil hota hai.

Reactive system hota hai

Normally institutions issue tab dekhte hain jab bill aa jata hai ya overconsumption ho chuka hota hai. Ye platform proactive planning allow karta hai.

Management visibility kam hoti hai

Upper management ko clear answer chahiye hota hai: kya consume ho raha hai, kya बचत ho sakti hai, aur kya next action hona chahiye.

3

5

Management summary, screenshot mode, print report aur recommendation tracking ki wajah se decision se execution tak flow complete hota hai.

AI Logic

Isme AI exactly kya karta hai?

Forecasting

System future resource demand estimate karta hai. Daily, monthly, yearly aur seasonal modes available hain.

Occupancy-aware prediction

Agar June ya vacation months mein students kam hain, to system lower demand planning dikhata hai. Agar busy academic period hai, to demand high expect karta hai.

Risk Engine

Growth, variability aur trend ke basis par high, medium ya low risk type insight deta hai.

Recommendations

System sirf numbers nahi dikhata, balki action suggestions bhi deta hai, jaise saving opportunities, unusual spikes aur planning interventions.

Governance

Control aur transparency kahan milti hai?

Editable assumptions

Energy cost per unit, carbon factor, occupancy values aur benchmark values ko adjust kiya ja sakta hai.

Building ranking

Kaun sa building better perform kar raha hai aur kaun sa attention demand kar raha hai, ye instantly dikh jata hai.

Action tracking

Recommendation ko suggested, planned, in progress ya completed status mein convert kiya ja sakta hai.

Plain-language explanation

Terms aur formulas ko simple language mein explain kiya gaya hai, taaki non-technical audience bhi samajh sake.
